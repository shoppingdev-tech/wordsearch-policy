Privacy Policy

Word Search

Effective date: 15 October 2026

In short: Word Search does not collect, send or sell your information. It has no accounts, ads, analytics, tracking or in-app purchases. Your progress and settings stay on your device.

Who we are

This policy explains how the Word Search mobile game (the "App"), published by [DEVELOPER NAME] ("we", "us"), handles information. It applies to the App on iOS and Android.

Information we collect

None. The App does not ask you to create an account and does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, camera, location or microphone. It does not send your game data or any information about you or your device to us or to anyone else. The App does not include advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking software.

Game data on your device

To let you continue where you left off, the App saves the following only on your device:

  • Your game progress: solved levels, stars, the last level you played, the daily puzzle and your daily streak
  • Your token balance (tokens are earned by playing and used for hints; they cannot be bought, exchanged for money or transferred)
  • Your settings: language, music, sound effects, vibration, animations and daily reminder time
  • Whether you have seen the tutorial and introduction screens

This data never leaves your device. Deleting the App removes it permanently. Because we never receive it, we cannot restore it if you delete the App or change devices.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the App asks for permission to show notifications and schedules a local reminder on your device at the time you choose. Reminders are created on your device, not sent from a server. You can turn them off in the App's settings or in your device settings at any time.

Device checks

To keep the game fair, the App checks on your device whether it appears to be rooted, jailbroken or modified. This check runs entirely on your device; the result is not stored or sent anywhere. The App also reads its own version number to show it in Settings.

Advertising and purchases

The App shows no advertising and offers no in-app purchases.

Children's privacy

The App is suitable for all ages. It does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your country).

Third-party content

The background music, "Children's March Theme" by CleytonKauffman, is used under the CC0 public-domain dedication. It is bundled inside the App and does not involve any data sharing.

Your rights

We hold no account, profile or other data about you. All game data stays on your device, and you can remove it at any time by deleting the App.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the App handles information, we will update this page and the effective date above before the change takes effect.
