Privacy Policy

Word Search

Effective date: 16 October 2026

In short: Word Search does not sell your information and has no accounts, analytics or tracking. It shows an ad only when you choose to watch one to get a hint (ads by Google AdMob). The App needs an internet connection. Your game progress and basic device information are saved on our server under an anonymous device identifier, so your progress is kept if you reinstall. Purchases are handled by Apple or Google and checked through RevenueCat (our processor). Only if you choose to use "Create" are the photos or description you add sent securely to our puzzle service (Google acts as our processor) to build your word search puzzle. Photos are not stored, and the puzzles you create are saved on our server under your device identifier.

Who we are

This policy explains how the Word Search mobile game (the "App"), published by Paresh ("we", "us"), handles information. It applies to the App on iOS and Android.

Information we collect

The App does not ask you to create an account and does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location or microphone. It uses your camera or photo library only if you choose to add a photo to a puzzle you are creating, and only for the photos you pick (see "Create your own puzzle").

To save your progress, the App sends the following to our server over an encrypted connection:

  • An anonymous device identifier: on Android the app-specific Android ID, on iOS a random ID kept in the device Keychain. It is not linked to your name or any account.
  • Device information: brand, model, device name, operating system and version, whether it is a tablet or emulator, mobile carrier and memory size
  • App information: app version, language, country, locale and time zone
  • Your game progress and settings (listed below), and when you first and last opened the App

We use this only to keep and restore your progress and to keep the App working. It is not sold and not used for advertising. Apart from the optional Create feature and the optional ads described below, it is not shared with third parties. The App does not include analytics, crash-reporting or tracking software.

Game data

To let you continue where you left off, the App saves the following on your device and on our server (puzzles in progress stay on your device only):

  • Your game progress: solved levels, stars, saved puzzles in progress and your daily streak
  • Your token balance (tokens are earned by playing or bought in the App and are used for hints and for creating puzzles; they cannot be exchanged for money or transferred)
  • The puzzles you create with "Create" and their details (see "Create your own puzzle")
  • Your settings: music, sound effects, vibration, animations and daily reminder time, and whether you have seen the how-to-play tutorial

Deleting the App removes the copy on your device. The copy on our server is kept so a reinstall can restore your progress; ask us to delete it at any time (see "Your rights").

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the App asks for permission to show notifications and schedules a local reminder on your device at the time you choose. Reminders are created on your device, not sent from a server. You can turn them off in the App's settings or in your device settings at any time.

Device checks

To keep the game fair, the App checks on your device whether it appears to be rooted, jailbroken or modified. This check runs entirely on your device; the result is not stored or sent anywhere. The App also reads its own version number to show it in Settings.

When you use "Create", the App also asks Google (Play Integrity on Android) or Apple (App Attest on iOS) for a short proof that it is a genuine copy of the App on a genuine device, and sends that proof to our server with your request. Our server checks it with Google or Apple to block automated misuse, and keeps only the result of the check and its time. It contains no personal information about you.

In-app purchases

You can buy token packs in the App. Payments are processed entirely by Apple's App Store or Google Play under their own privacy policies; we never see or store your payment card details. Purchases are checked through RevenueCat, Inc., which acts as our processor: the App and our server share with it only your anonymous device identifier and the purchase itself (which pack, the store's order or transaction identifier, the price, currency and country as reported by the store, and its date). We keep a record of each purchase under your device identifier to add the tokens to your balance exactly once and to handle refunds: if a purchase is refunded, its tokens are taken back.

Ads (optional)

When you don't have enough tokens for a hint, you can choose to watch an ad to get it instead. The App shows no other ads. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show an ad, measure it and prevent fraud, Google receives information from your device such as your IP address, device type and operating system, app information, the advertising identifier on Android, and how you interact with the ad. Our own server receives nothing about the ads; the App only counts on your device how many hints you got from ads today.

Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and some US states), Google's consent form asks for your choice before any ad is requested, and you can change it at any time in Settings under "Ad privacy choices". On iOS the App never asks to track you, so ads there are not personalised. Ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences. Google describes how it uses this information at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Create your own puzzle (optional)

If you choose to use "Create", you can add up to 3 photos and/or type a short description (for example "dinosaurs" or "my trip to Paris") to build a word search puzzle. Nothing is sent until you tap to build the puzzle, and the first time you use Create the App asks you to agree to what is described here. If you never use this feature, no photos or descriptions are uploaded.

  • What is sent. The photos and description are sent over an encrypted connection to our puzzle service. Before anything is sent on, our server checks that each file is a real image, removes hidden data from it, including EXIF details and any location information, and makes it smaller.
  • Who processes it. To build the puzzle, our server passes the photos and description to Google (its Gemini service), which acts as our processor. It is used only to create your puzzle and to check that the content is allowed, not to identify you and not for advertising. Under our terms with Google, it is not used to train Google's models; Google may keep requests for a short time to detect misuse, as set out in its terms.
  • What we keep. We do not store your photos or your description. We store the finished puzzle: its title, words, letter grid, level, size and creation date, together with your anonymous device identifier. This is what lets you replay your puzzles and get them back after a reinstall. We also keep how many puzzles you have created per day and month to apply fair-use limits, and the result of the device check described above.
  • Tokens. Creating a puzzle costs tokens. If a puzzle cannot be built, the tokens are returned to your balance.
  • Automatic creation. The puzzle is built automatically, so its words are not reviewed by a person and can occasionally be odd or wrong. Please do not add photos of people, children or personal documents.

Deleting your puzzles. You can delete any puzzle you created in "My puzzles" in the App. This removes it from our server (tokens are not refunded). To have all of your puzzles and the other data stored for your device deleted, contact us (see "Your rights"). Because we do not keep your photos or description, there is nothing to delete for those.

Using Create needs an internet connection.

Purchases and "Create" are subject to the parental controls of the App Store or Google Play. Ads are only shown when you choose to watch one, and only ads rated for general audiences.

Your rights

We hold no account or named profile about you. You can ask us for a copy of the game and device data stored for your device, including the puzzles you created, or ask us to delete it, by contacting us at the address below. You can remove the data stored on your device at any time by deleting the App, and delete individual created puzzles in "My puzzles". If you used "Create", you can also ask us about the photos and descriptions you sent; we do not keep them, but we will tell you what we hold.
